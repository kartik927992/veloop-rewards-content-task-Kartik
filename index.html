VELOOP Rewards

1. Platform Introduction

What is VELOOP Rewards?

VELOOP Rewards is a rewards and engagement platform where you can earn digital rewards simply by staying active every day. Instead of relying on just one way to earn, VELOOP brings together several features, from watching ads to daily streaks, referrals, and fun mini-games, so there's always something new to try.

Every activity you complete adds VEs (VELOOP Earn Tokens) to your account. These can later be redeemed based on the platform's redemption policies. Whether you have five minutes or an hour to spare, VELOOP makes it easy to turn everyday actions into real rewards.

2. Registration & Authentication

Getting started with VELOOP Rewards takes less than a minute.

Email Registration: enter your name, email, and a password, verify your email, and you're in.

Google Sign-In: one tap, no new password to remember, and your account is secured through Google's authentication.

Logging in is just as flexible. You can use Google, your email and password, or email plus OTP where available.

Forgot your password? No stress:

  1. Tap "Forgot Password"
  2. Enter your registered email
  3. You'll get an OTP or verification email
  4. Verify your identity
  5. Set a new password
  6. Log back in

Keeping your account safe: every password needs at least 8 characters, an uppercase and lowercase letter, a number, and a special character. Behind the scenes, VELOOP uses encrypted authentication and OTP verification to keep your account protected, so you can focus on earning instead of worrying.

3. Dashboard

Once you log in, the Dashboard is your home base. It's a quick snapshot of your account, not a page crammed with everything at once.

From here you can see your Available VEs and your Available Balance (an estimate of your redeemable value), plus a shortcut straight to your Wallet for transaction history and redemption options.

The Dashboard's real job is navigation. It's your gateway to every earning feature, each with its own dedicated space: Watch Ads, Daily Streak, Referral, Mine & Earn, Stake & Earn, Tap & Earn, Contribution, Giveaway, Profile, and Wallet. Instead of overwhelming you with everything at once, VELOOP keeps each activity in its own clean, focused screen, so you always know where you are and what to do next.

4. VE Wallet, VEs & SVEs

Your Wallet is where everything comes together: balances, history, and redemption, all in one place.

What are VEs?

VEs (VELOOP Earn Tokens) are VELOOP's main reward currency. You earn them by watching ads, keeping your daily streak alive, referring friends, tapping and mining, catching daily bonuses, and joining campaigns or seasonal events. Once earned, VEs can be redeemed through the platform's available reward options.

What are SVEs?

SVEs (Silver VEs) are a secondary currency. They can't be redeemed directly. Instead, they convert into VEs first, and from there become eligible for redemption. Conversion rates aren't fixed. They can shift with campaigns or platform updates, so it's always worth checking the wallet for the latest numbers.

Understanding redemption

Because VELOOP supports several redemption options, the number of VEs needed changes depending on what you're redeeming. There's no single flat exchange rate. For reference, at the highest UPI tier, roughly 150 VEs is estimated to be worth ₹1, but this is just a rough guide, not a guarantee. Rewards like Amazon or Google Play gift cards each have their own requirements too. Your Wallet always shows the most accurate, up to date numbers.

Your Wallet lets you:

  • Track your earnings
  • Review your reward history
  • Check redemption options
  • See past transactions
  • Estimate your reward value
  • Get eligible rewards ready to redeem

FAQs

Can I earn VEs every day?

Yes. Daily activities like watching ads, keeping your streak, and referring friends all add to your VE balance.

Are VEs transferable?

Not currently. VEs stay tied to your account. If that changes in future, it'll be announced through official updates.

Do VEs expire?

No, not just from inactivity. VEs can only be deducted or revoked if an account is found violating VELOOP's Terms of Service or misusing the rewards system. This is a safeguard to keep things fair for everyone.
