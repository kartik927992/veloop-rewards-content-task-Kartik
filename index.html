4. VE Wallet, VEs & SVEs

Your Wallet is where everything comes together: balances, history, and reward redemption, all in one place.

What are VEs?

VEs (VELOOP Earn Tokens) are VELOOP's main reward currency. You earn them by watching ads to earn money, keeping your daily streak alive, referring friends, tapping and mining, catching daily bonuses, and joining campaigns or seasonal events. Once earned, VEs can be redeemed through the platform's available reward options.

What are SVEs?

SVEs (Silver VEs) are a secondary currency. They can't be redeemed directly. Instead, they convert into VEs first, and from there become eligible for redemption. Conversion rates aren't fixed. They can shift with campaigns or platform updates, so it's always worth checking the wallet for the latest numbers.

FAQs

Can I earn VEs every day?

Yes. Daily activities like watching ads, keeping your streak, and referring friends all add to your VE balance making VELOOP a reliable daily rewards app.

Are VEs transferable?

Not currently. VEs stay tied to your account. If that changes in future, it'll be announced through official updates.

Do VEs expire?

No, not just from inactivity. VEs can only be deducted or revoked if an account is found violating VELOOP's Terms of Service or misusing the rewards system. This is a safeguard to keep things fair for everyone.
