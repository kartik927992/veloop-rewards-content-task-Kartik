## 2. Registration & Authentication

Getting started with VELOOP Rewards — a **secure rewards app** takes less than a minute.

**Two ways to sign up:**

- **Email Registration:** enter your name, email, and a password, verify your email, and you're in.
- **Google Sign-In:** one tap, no new password to remember, and your account is secured through Google's authentication.

Logging in is just as flexible. You can use Google, your email and password, or email plus OTP where available — all part of VELOOP's **secure login system**.

### Forgot your password? No stress:

1. Tap "Forgot Password"
2. Enter your registered email
3. You'll get an OTP or verification email
4. Verify your identity
5. Set a new password
6. Log back in

Keeping your account safe: every password needs at least 8 characters, an uppercase and lowercase letter, a number, and a special character. Behind the scenes, VELOOP uses **encrypted authentication** and OTP verification to keep your account protected, so you can focus on earning instead of worrying.

## 4. VE Wallet, VEs & SVEs

Your Wallet is where everything comes together: balances, history, and **reward redemption**, all in one place.

### What are VEs?

VEs (VELOOP Earn Tokens) are VELOOP's main **reward currency**. You earn them by **watching ads to earn money**, keeping your daily streak alive, referring friends, tapping and mining, catching daily bonuses, and joining campaigns or seasonal events. Once earned, VEs can be redeemed through the platform's available **reward options**.

### What are SVEs?

SVEs (Silver VEs) are a secondary currency. They can't be redeemed directly. Instead, they convert into VEs first, and from there become eligible for redemption. Conversion rates aren't fixed. They can shift with campaigns or platform updates, so it's always worth checking the wallet for the latest numbers.

### Understanding redemption

Because VELOOP supports several **redemption options**  including **redeem gift cards** like Amazon and Google Play  the number of VEs needed changes depending on what you're redeeming. There's no single flat exchange rate. For example, UPI withdrawals require different VE amounts for different payout values:

| Withdrawal Amount | Required VEs |
|---|---|
| ₹10 | 2,400 VEs |
| ₹25 | 5,800 VEs |
| ₹50 | 10,000 VEs |
| ₹100 | 19,500 VEs |
| ₹150 | 28,500 VEs |
| ₹300 | 52,500 VEs |
| ₹500 | 80,500 VEs |
| ₹1,000 | 150,000 VEs |

As a rough reference only, the highest UPI tier works out to roughly 150 VEs per ₹1  this is just an estimate, not a guarantee. Your Wallet always shows the most accurate, up-to-date numbers.

### Your Wallet lets you:

- Check redemption options
- See past transactions

- Get eligible rewards ready to redeem

## FAQs

**Can I earn VEs every day?**
Yes. Daily activities like watching ads, keeping your streak, and referring friends all add to your VE balance  making VELOOP a reliable **daily rewards app**.

**Are VEs transferable?**
Not currently. VEs stay tied to your account. If that changes in future, it'll be announced through official updates.

**Do VEs expire?**
No, not just from inactivity. VEs can only be deducted or revoked if an account is found violating VELOOP's Terms of Service or misusing the rewards system. This is a safeguard to keep things fair for everyone.
