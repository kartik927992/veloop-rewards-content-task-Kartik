2. INTRODUCTION

The Get Paid To (GPT) industry is made up of platforms that give people chances to earn rewards by doing different digital tasks. These tasks can be surveys, offers, app downloads, games, advertisements, micro tasks, referrals and other online activities.

GPT platforms usually work by linking users with advertisers, market research companies offer providers and other business partners. Users finish the tasks while the platform earns money from its partners and gives a part of that money back to users as rewards.

VELoop Rewards is being created inside this industry. Therefore understanding the GPT platforms is important for spotting successful features earning methods, reward systems, user experience practices and monetization strategies.

This research looks at selected GPT platforms to learn how they work and what features could be taken or improved for VELoop Rewards.
